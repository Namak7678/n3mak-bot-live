# N3mak AI — business assistant bot for small companies

Try it: https://t.me/N3mak_bot

An AI assistant on Telegram for small business owners. It answers customer questions, drafts emails and documents, and plans automation work. Free to use.

Inside the bot:
- Free AI answers in any language
- 10 AI Automation Blueprints (PDF, 450 Telegram Stars) — ready-to-sell automations with prompts, stack, deploy time and pricing
- Monthly automation services for companies

## Why this exists

Most small companies do not need a full-time hire for repetitive work. They need one assistant that answers instantly and one clear plan for what to automate first. This bot does both.

## Stack

Node.js, no dependencies. Telegram Bot API webhook, OpenRouter free models, Base44 entity for sales records.

## Run locally

```
TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN=... OPENROUTER_API_KEY=... node bot.js
```
